# Analysis workflow

## Source status

Record `input_type`, `source_id`, `transcript_status`, `language`, `acquisition_method`, `terms_status`, `commercial_use_status`, and `text_hash`. Never claim a transcript was inspected without a successful lawful input.

## Eight analysis fields

1. **Topic** — event, entity, audience task, freshness, decision relevance.
2. **Title** — promise, mechanism, specificity, tension, evidence visibility, overclaim risk.
3. **Hook** — first event, conflict, audience payoff, uncertainty, time to thesis.
4. **Copy structure** — chapter order, information release, transitions, suspense, counterpoint.
5. **Evidence** — source type, placement, attribution, evidence-before-opinion or opinion-before-evidence.
6. **Viewpoint** — thesis strength, fact/inference/opinion separation, uncertainty language.
7. **Rhythm** — sentence length, question ratio, oral density, reset frequency, emotional curve.
8. **Ending** — conclusion type, next verification point, risk boundary, CTA intensity.

For each field output:

- `status`: `OBSERVED | INFERRED | UNAVAILABLE`
- `finding`
- `evidence_pointer`
- `confidence`: `HIGH | MEDIUM | LOW`
- `transferable_mechanism`
- `do_not_copy`

## Aggregation

Aggregate only recurring observations supported by at least three source videos. Keep exceptions visible. Distinguish packaging frequency from performance correlation, and never infer causation from views alone.

## Composite rule

Blend functions, not surface identity. Good combinations include:

- decision compression + data-led evidence + macro-to-micro ending;
- factual event Hook + consensus challenge + conditional watchlist;
- three-item update + one common thesis + evidence-first close.

Do not reuse creator names, portraits, program names, catchphrases, fixed intros, unique metaphors, or personal anecdotes.
